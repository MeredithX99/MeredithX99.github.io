Terminology

NAME INTRODUCTION
Dance Image The dynamic image of the character shaped by the means of dance art, the posture, modeling, footwork and other movements of the human body with the help of music, stage art, makeup, clothing and other artistic factors produce visual effects with appreciation value. It is the main object that is perceived and recognized in the process of dance appreciation and the main body that embodies the aesthetic value of dance art.
Dance Expression According to the psychological activities of people in real life and the habit of showing expressions, through refining and artistic processing, with different dance forms to summarize and show the inner emotional changes such as joy, sorrow and joy. In addition to facial expressions coordinated with movements, rhythmic movements, postures, gestures and shapes can also produce artistic dance expressions.
Dance Movement The basic means of expression of dance art. It is derived from the imitation of human emotional movements and all kinds of dynamic things in nature.

Body Positions

NAME INTRODUCTION
Arabesque A position of the body in which a dancer stands on one leg with the other leg raised behind the body, extended backward in a straight line.
Attitude A position of the body which is a variation of the arabesque. In this position, a dancer's extended leg is raised behind the body, bent at the knee at a 90-degree angle.
Assemblé A jump in which a dancer leaves the ground on one leg, and lands on the ground on two legs. The dancer's legs come together at the same time and return to fifth position.
Grand jeté A large jump or leap from one foot to the other. In this movement, the raised foot moves forward and then is thrown back as the jump is made, with the landing on the raised foot.
Turnout A body position in which the dancer turns his or her feet and legs out from the hip joints.
Pirouette A complete, 360-degree spin or turn of the body on one foot, on point or half-point.
Plié A bending of the knee or knees.
